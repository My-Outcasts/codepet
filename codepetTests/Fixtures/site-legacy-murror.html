How it works

Three steps, every day

1

Name what you feel

Say it badly, in your own words. Murror helps you find the more accurate word for it.

2

See the pattern

The same feeling keeps arriving on the same kind of day. That is the useful part.

3

Reach out

One small, specific message to one real person — written by you, unstuck by us.
